Our Journey

Clinical Milestones & Evolution

From our founding as an intimate facial balancing practice to our position as a leader in non-surgical aesthetics.

2018

Foundation of Aurelle Aesthetics

Founded by Dr. Elena Vance, MD, with three private clinical treatment rooms dedicated to conservative neuromodulator micro-dosing and natural facial architecture.

2020

Vascular Ultrasound Integration

Became an early adopter of point-of-care high-frequency ultrasound vascular pre-mapping, virtually eliminating vascular occlusion risks during complex filler procedures.

2022

Advanced Energy & Laser Center

Expanded facility to include FDA-cleared Morpheus8 radiofrequency microneedling and dual-wave hybrid fractional laser technologies for subdermal tightening.

2024

Autologous Cellular Rejuvenation

Introduced autologous PRP growth factor treatments and personalized barrier recovery skincare regimens, uniting in-clinic procedures with home skin health.

2026

Digital Sanctuary & Multilingual Care

Launched our seamless digital patient portal, bidirectional layout navigation (LTR/RTL), and expanded multilingual specialist consultations.
