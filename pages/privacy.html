Patient Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy & HIPAA Standards

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Discretion

At Aurelle Aesthetics, we uphold the highest medical and ethical standards for patient confidentiality. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, safeguard, and utilize information gathered through our Beverly Hills clinic sanctuary and our demonstration web portal.

2. Medical & Clinical Information (HIPAA Compliance)

All protected health information (PHI) collected during medical consultations, diagnostic skin analyses, ultrasound anatomical mapping, and procedural treatment histories is maintained in strict compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We never sell, monetize, or publicly disclose patient health data.

3. Web Portal & Demonstration Data

Our web application uses local browser session storage for demonstration appointments, client portal access, and theme preferences. In this demonstration environment, no real payment transactions or unsecured sensitive credentials are stored or transmitted.

4. Photography & Clinical Documentation

Any anatomical baseline photographs taken during clinical assessments are encrypted and stored within our secure electronic medical records (EMR) system. Photographs are never utilized for marketing or educational purposes without prior, explicit, written patient authorization.
